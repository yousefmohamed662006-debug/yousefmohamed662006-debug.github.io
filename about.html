About me

I care about understanding why a solution works.

I am an Artificial Intelligence student focused on building practical Machine Learning systems and explaining the engineering decisions behind them.

My current focus

Through the FlyRank Machine Learning and AI Fluency tracks, I have worked on task framing, data contracts, baseline ranking rules, grouped model evaluation, prompt systems, and evidence-led portfolio communication.

Alongside my studies at Suez Canal University, I develop computer vision models and neural network regression pipelines for industrial paint analysis at Kapci Coatings. I prioritize reproducibility, leak-free validation, and measurable operational value over complex hype.

Working principles
1

Start with the decision

Define what someone needs to do differently before choosing a model architecture.

2

Make the baseline visible

Use a simple benchmark so later complexity has something honest to beat.

3

Protect evaluation

Check leakage, split integrity, feature timing, and operational metric fit.

4

Explain the limits

Separate measured evidence from assumptions, proxies, and next steps.
