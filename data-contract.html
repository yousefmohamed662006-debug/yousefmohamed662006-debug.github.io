Data Engineering & Hygiene Case Study

Defining what warehouse data actually means before modeling.

Before training models, I documented the row unit, time windows, field meanings, missingness semantics, verification queries, and anti-leakage boundaries for 30,000 multi-client search records.

Why the Data Contract Mattered

A clean-looking SQL table can silently destroy a machine learning model if grain, time window, or source availability flags are misinterpreted. This contract converted a large warehouse release into explicit, verifiable assumptions before feature extraction began.

Core Definitions & Grain

  • Unit of Analysis: Exactly one content page for one reporting month and client organization.
  • Source Families: Google Search Console (GSC), Google Analytics 4 (GA4), channel traffic sessions, and emerging AI referral sources.
  • Zero Interpretation Flags: Fields such as client_has_gsc and client_has_ga4 distinguish between genuine zero traffic and unintegrated tracking.

Representative Feature Groups

Search Visibility

gsc_impressions, gsc_clicks, gsc_sum_position, and gsc_avg_position.

Engagement Signals

ga4_pageviews, ga4_sessions, ga4_users, and ga4_engaged_sessions.

Channel Mix

Organic search, direct, referral, social, paid, and AI-attributed traffic shares.

AI Referral Sources

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, and Meta referral indicators.

Automated Verification Pipeline

I designed a 5-step automated SQL and pandas verification suite before model training:

  1. Confirm expected reporting month and distinct primary key grain.
  2. Detect and eliminate duplicate page-client-month combinations.
  3. Validate null and zero patterns against integration availability flags.
  4. Verify that derived rate denominators are strictly positive and bounded.
  5. Confirm that candidate features are historically available at recommendation time.

Leakage Traps Documented & Prevented

Candidate features were strictly selected from information available prior to the decision window. Target-derived fields, future rolling metrics, client-specific IDs, and post-audit tags were rigorously excluded from the modeling matrix.

Documented Anti-Pattern: A feature often appears highly predictive simply because it was calculated during or after the outcome window. High correlation is not evidence of predictive power—temporal availability must be strictly guaranteed.

Measurable Outcome

The primary outcome was a hardened, reproducible data foundation. By defining explicit contracts and automated verification scripts, the subsequent Logistic Regression model achieved 0.74 Precision@50 with zero silent data corruption or post-split leakage.
